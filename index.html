About

Kevin Camacho — Chicago.

I run Project.01, a one-person studio making video advertising and photo + video for Chicago's small businesses and real estate listings.

Marketing is my day job and my background. I'm a Business Development Representative at Groupon, where I work with local beauty and wellness businesses — building their campaigns, structuring their deals, negotiating pricing, and getting them live and selling. In Q3 2026, I drove $69,322 in gross profit across 52 merchants launched — at that pace, over $277,000 a year for small businesses through Groupon. I know what makes a local offer actually sell, because I build offers for a living.

Before Groupon, I worked in Chicago real estate as a leasing agent — at Fulton Grace Realty and at Becovic Management Group. I showed apartments across the city, worked with landlords and renters, and learned what actually makes a place lease: the light, the layout, the first photo. That's what I bring to real estate media now. I shoot listings the way a leasing agent sees them, because I've been the one standing in the unit with the renter.

Photography is the other half. I've been shooting for about ten years — street photography, mostly, under B.W.PROJECT — and I published my first photography book, Apollo. My current book, In Between, is in progress.

Project.01 is where the two meet: marketing that knows how to sell, and photography that knows how to see. Real estate photo + video, product, and short-form advertising for shops, salons, restaurants, and local services — made to move, not just to look good.
